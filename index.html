MeterBot · AI billing automation

Fair, per-minute billing for a driver-services operator

A Hyderabad-based driver-and-services operator was losing time and trust to manual billing. Fares were rounded to the nearest half-hour, receipts were handwritten, and disputes over "why am I paying for a full hour?" were common. Drivers spent minutes at the end of every job doing mental math.

We implemented an AI-assisted billing engine — MeterBot — that tracks worked time automatically, keeps the first hour as a fair fixed minimum, then bills every additional minute pro-rata. A live "Billed so far" meter ticks up in real time, and each job ends with a clear, itemised receipt in both English and Telugu.

"Now customers see exactly what they're paying for, as it happens. The arguments at the end of the trip just stopped." — Operations lead, driver-services operator (representative engagement)
