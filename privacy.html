Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how VarniksGlobal LLP (“Varniks Global”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you visit varniksglobal.com or contact us by email or phone.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message. The form opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing reaches us until you send that email, and this website does not store what you type.
  • Email and phone: your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us when you write to or call us, including details about your business or project.
  • Technical data: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may process your IP address and browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies.

We do not set cookies or use analytics on this website.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss our products and services.
  • To prepare for and carry out work you ask us to do.
  • To keep business, accounting and tax records.
  • To meet our legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

3. We do not sell your data

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. When we share information

We share information only where needed: with service providers who support our operations (such as email and hosting providers), with professional advisers, and where the law or a competent authority requires it.

5. How long we keep information

We keep enquiries and related correspondence for as long as needed to deal with them and to maintain the relationship, and for longer where legal, tax or accounting rules require it.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we hold. No email or internet transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information through email.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent you have given, subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. To do so, write to hello@varniksglobal.com.

8. Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Our products and other websites

This policy covers this website and business enquiries. A product of ours may have its own privacy policy, which applies to your use of that product. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites we link to.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
